## What the finalizer actually derives

`app/live_manifest_finalizer.py` (under `moss_transcribe_diarize/`):
`LiveManifestRetune` takes optional **explicit bytes**. `_tape_bytes` reads that
value; it requires a positive integer divisible by frame bytes and at least
`max_retained_samples * 2`. It does not derive tape size from the rolling ring.
`validate_contract` derives seconds for reporting as `bytes / (16,000 * 2)`.
Omission means no complete tape. Increasing tape capacity does not require raising
the rolling ring; the inequality is a minimum tape size, not equality.
