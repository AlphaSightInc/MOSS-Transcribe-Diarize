Frozen decision
===============
- Runtime: one local FastAPI process, real MOSS decoder, explicit local Hugging Face model, real
  human speech at 0.5-second wall-clock cadence, descriptor-derived geometry only.
- Candidate normal-session counts: 2 and 4; screen each for 120 seconds, then soak only the largest
  passing count for 600 seconds.
- Gates: maximum session p95 transcript lag 10.0 seconds (linear Type-7), maximum local process-tree
  RSS growth 4 GiB from warm idle, zero OOM/accelerator errors, maximum fair-dispatch skew 1.
- Overload: one v2 429 must leave the saturated session non-terminal and unconsumed, allow its peer,
  then accept the retried frame once capacity is available.
- Integrity: distinct speech markers must remain isolated through overload and one cursor-based
  observer reconnect per session. The subsequent runner must retain all declared raw arrays.
