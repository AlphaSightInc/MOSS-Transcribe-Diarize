Iteration 9 — W2 CPU/HF-local runner and pre-measurement refreeze

Question
========
Can the next iteration make a real, resumable local CPU/HF measurement at the actual deployed
descriptor geometry without allowing it to be mistaken for deployed-GPU evidence?

Change
======
Added `run_cpu_hf_local_measurement.py` and a hash-pinned real-speech fixture. The runner reads the
deployed descriptor read-only; re-finalizes only the local manifest immediately before starting one
loopback FastAPI process; sends real 0.5-second v2 PCM pairs plus helper health; and incrementally
records frame responses, observer cursors/events, process-tree RSS, canonical dispatch order, local
decode RTF, per-session queue depth, overload/retry, and reconnect evidence. Capture and view bearer
values are not persisted.

The frozen 1/2/4/8 matrix replaces the insufficient 2/4-only matrix before any result exists:

    sha256=955a2883ada6cef99be007d1eb3c9838d6be9e227dc1408e06b6f8617fdb9d6c

The contract records why the 10.0-second p95 and 4-GiB RSS ceilings are deliberately-wide safety
headroom, requires comparison with the prior 0.248-second CPU p95, and names the unavailable
deployed GPU p95/GPU OOM/GPU memory-vLLM-count/deployed-RTF figures explicitly.

The initial per-event `fsync` would have measured its own I/O under the canonical publication lock.
It was replaced before a measurement with record construction under the short lock, serialization and
enqueue after it, and a dedicated writer thread with a single long-lived handle. That writer syncs on
a five-second interval and shutdown; its enqueue/write/sync/pending-record costs are emitted with each
phase. This preserves raw incremental artifacts without putting disk latency into dispatch fairness.
