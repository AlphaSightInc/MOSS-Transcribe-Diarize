Iteration 05 — reachable pre-frame state search

Question
========

Can the queue-depth probe maximize a 40,000-sample client frame over every
legitimate EndpointPolicy state and WebRTC VAD carry state, rather than only a
fresh provider/policy pair?

Change
======

`probe_queue_depth_floor.py --search-reachable-worst-case` now models a client
split at every possible sample in a configured WebRTC VAD frame.  It keeps the
previous VAD answer until the split completes, then applies the new answer,
which matches `WebRtcSpeechProvider.observe()`.  Its transition cache
materializes each unique relative state and executes the production
`EndpointPolicy` for cache misses.  Its final witness reconstructs the history
with the production WebRTC adapter and calls
`LiveCoordinator.preview_frame_work_items()`.

Unresolved boundary
===================

The aggressive 40,000-sample command was deliberately interrupted after three
30-second measurement intervals while still constructing reachable boundary
states.  It produced no JSON artifact and no queue-depth floor.  That is not a
failed safety assertion and must not be read as a 166- or 25-item all-state
result.  The next probe must add a bounded progress/state-count artifact or a
provably equivalent dominance reduction before attempting a config-load guard.
