Question: can the carry-aware search replace its sample-at-a-time reachability
walk with direct enumeration of each VAD split point without losing the
intermediate state merging that makes the toy geometry tractable?

Temporary change (reverted before this artifact was committed):
  _reachable_boundary_states enumerated each `carried_samples` split as one
  carried-prefix transition plus one frame-completing suffix transition.

Validation command:
  env PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python \
    evidence/phase1/y5-guardrail-and-floor/probe_queue_depth_floor.py \
    --frame-samples 160 --vad-frame-samples 160 \
    --min-speech-samples 160 --min-silence-samples 320 \
    --hard-cap-samples 1600 --search-reachable-worst-case \
    --json-output /tmp/iteration-06-reachable-toy.json

Observed result:
  The direct-split version did not finish the one-VAD-frame toy command or
  create its JSON.  The first invocation was observed at 01:31 elapsed; an
  accidental duplicate was at 00:44.  Both were our local probe processes and
  were stopped with SIGTERM before any result was written.

Verdict:
  REJECTED and reverted.  The direct form is transition-equivalent, but it
  delays equivalence merging until a VAD-frame boundary and therefore creates
  the cross product of the current state set and all 160 split positions.  The
  retained sample-at-a-time walk merges equivalent intermediate states after
  each sample; its existing toy result is
  iteration-05-reachable-toy.json.  A future reduction must preserve that
  intermediate merge point or first record bounded state-count progress.
